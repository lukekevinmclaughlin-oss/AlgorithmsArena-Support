# Algorithms Arena

Algorithms Arena is a native algorithms and data-structures learning app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Support

If you need help with the app, purchases, progress, or accessibility, use the [support contact form](https://www.lukekevinmclaughlin.com/resume#request-cv). Please include “Algorithms Arena” in your message. Purchase refunds and subscription management are handled by Apple through your Apple Account.

## Privacy Policy

Effective: July 22, 2026

Algorithms Arena does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, or precise location. The app contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, accounts, or third-party trackers.

Lesson progress, drill attempts, review scheduling, ratings, settings, and a local purchase-state cache remain on your device. You can export a readable copy of your learning data from Settings. The app’s course content and core learning features work offline.

If you choose to use in-app purchases or Game Center, those features are provided by Apple under your Apple Account. Algorithms Arena does not operate a server and does not receive your Apple Account credentials. Game Center display names, purchase history, subscription management, and refund handling are governed by Apple’s policies.

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Privacy questions can be sent through the [support contact form](https://www.lukekevinmclaughlin.com/resume#request-cv).
